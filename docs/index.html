DepthMotion

Monocular 3D Perception and Short-Term Trajectory Forecasting

A monocular computer vision pipeline that estimates object positions in 3D, tracks their motion and predicts short-term trajectories from RGB video.

RESEARCH QUESTION

Can monocular depth estimation provide sufficiently useful 3D spatial information for short-term trajectory forecasting in a real-time vision system?

The project investigates how errors in monocular depth estimation propagate through 3D localization, motion estimation and trajectory forecasting.

EXPERIMENTS

Depth evaluation

Metric depth was evaluated against Virtual KITTI 2 ground truth to quantify the spatial uncertainty entering the trajectory pipeline.

4.01 m MAE
6.06 m RMSE
13.14% AbsRel

KEY FINDING

Depth uncertainty increases substantially with distance.

Mean absolute depth error increases from approximately 0.66 m at 0–10 m to 7.64 m at 40–50 m. This creates distance-dependent uncertainty in downstream 3D motion estimation and forecasting.
